Everything

Every section of the site, in one place. The unfinished ones are marked rather than hidden — they exist in the data, but their pages aren't built yet.

  • competitions — every league and cup, 1884 to present
  • teams — every team with a game on record
    • all-time club records — every club's record, most wins first
    • seasons — every season and the competitions that ran in it
    • standings — every season standing on record
    • stats — every player-season stat line
    • a random team
  • players — the players, and everyone else in the record
    • salaries — published MLS salaries, 2007–2013
    • a random player
  • games — every game on record, oldest first
    • goals — every goal on record
    • lineups — every recorded appearance
    • a random game
  • dates — browse by year, month calendar, or day, 1866 to present
    • today's scoreboard
    • this day in history
  • awards — championships, MVPs, best XIs, the hall of fame
  • drafts — every MLS draft since 1996
  • places — where the games were played, and where the players came from
    • countries — every country played in and against
    • states — every state and province, busiest first
    • cities — every city with a game or a birthplace on record
    • stadiums — every ground with a game on record
  • organizations — the six confederations and their member countries
  • managers — who ran the bench, team by team
  • sources — where the games, stats, and news here come from
  • search — players and teams by name
  • about — what this site is and how big it's gotten
    • contact

Not built yet

  • events
  • graphs
  • levels
  • news
  • positions index (individual position pages and the managers index work)
  • transactions (page exists, no data loaded yet)
  • videos
