games is the games played and recorded in that season; games on record as not played are left out of it and of every share beside it. results, lineups, stats, attendance, venue and referee are the share of those games carrying a result, at least one recorded appearance, at least one player stat line, a crowd, a ground, and a referee.

goals is different: it is the share of the goals those games are recorded as having scored that are itemized as individual goals, so a season with every score but no scorers reads 0%. table is the standings held for the season — final where the closing table was transcribed, in-season where only dated tables from during the season are.

A dash means nothing on record, not zero; <1% means a handful of games rather than none. Hover any share for the counts behind it.
