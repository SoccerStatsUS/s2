ASL — American Soccer League, 1921–1933 The first professional golden age, built on mill-town teams; the Depression ended it. NASL — North American Soccer League, 1968–1984 Pelé, the Cosmos, and more ambition than the gate receipts could carry. MLS — Major League Soccer, 1996– The top flight since 1996, founded as the price of hosting the 1994 World Cup. NWSL — National Women's Soccer League, 2013– The third attempt at a women's national league, and the one that lasted. USOC — US Open Cup, 1914– Open to every team in the country since 1914, as the National Challenge Cup. USMNT — US Men's National Team Every international on record, from Newark in 1885 to today.
